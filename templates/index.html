Frequently Asked Questions

Cloud services helps clarify remote access, data security, multi-user support, and pricing before subscription.

01

What is Tally on Cloud and how is it different from regular Tally? ?

Tally on Cloud hosts your Tally software on a secure cloud server, allowing you to access it remotely from any device. Unlike traditional Tally, which runs locally on a single system, the cloud version supports multi-user access, remote login, and automatic backups.

02

Will my data be safe and secure on the cloud?

Yes, your data is protected with end-to-end encryption, firewall security, and regular backups. Reputed cloud providers also offer role-based access control to ensure only authorized users can view or edit financial records.

03

Can multiple users access Tally simultaneously from different locations?

Absolutely. Tally on Cloud supports multi-user access*, enabling teams in different locations to work on the same company data in real time—ideal for distributed accounting and finance teams.


* Multi-user access may depend on your Tally license type.
04

Do I need to install anything on my computer to use Tally on Cloud?

No heavy installation is required. You can access Tally via a secure remote desktop client or browser-based interface. Basic setup support is provided to ensure smooth onboarding.

05

What are the costs involved and is it scalable for growing businesses?

Pricing typically includes a monthly or annual subscription based on user count and storage needs. It’s highly scalable—resources can be upgraded as your business grows, without major infrastructure changes.
